Silicon pn junction

Electrons and holes are ordinary species. Thick lines are their species voltages Vi = μ̄i/(ziF), the quasi-Fermi levels; thin lines are their standard levels V°i, the band edges (in voltage units the conduction band sits below the valence band). The bias moves the p side's metal contact: forward bias splits the quasi-Fermi levels across the junction, and the carriers recombine as they cross, in the layers or at the contacts.

The lifetime control sets band-to-band recombination. With a long lifetime the minority carriers' diffusion length L far exceeds the 1.5 µm layers: they cross to the contacts and recombine there (a short diode). With a short one, L shrinks below the layer thickness and they recombine inside (a long diode). Shockley's law covers both, with the current J₀(eV/VT − 1) and J₀ = qni²[Dn/(NALn) coth(Wp/Ln) + Dp/(NDLp) coth(Wn/Lp)], plus the recombination in the depletion layer (dashed). It holds to a few percent until the current is large, where series resistance and rising injection bend the curve below it.
