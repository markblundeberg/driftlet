Silicon pn junction

Electrons and holes are ordinary species. Solid lines are their species voltages Vi = μ̄i/(ziF), the quasi-Fermi levels; dashed lines are their standard levels V°i, the band edges (in voltage units the conduction band sits below the valence band). The bias moves the p side's metal contact: forward bias splits the quasi-Fermi levels across the junction, and the carriers recombine as they cross.
