MOS capacitor: accumulation, depletion, inversion

p-type silicon under 5 nm of oxide and a metal gate: its capacitance against the ideal low-frequency C–V curve, and the bands bending at the surface, from accumulation through depletion to inversion. Vary the gate voltage and the doping. (More about the model below.)

About this simulation

The gate voltage sets the gate metal's Fermi level, and the silicon's bands bend at the surface to meet it. The band diagram uses the species voltage Vi = μ̄i/(ziF): a species' electrochemical potential per unit charge, in volts. For electrons and holes these are the quasi-Fermi levels (thick), drawn with their standard levels V°i, the band edges (thin). Negative bias pulls holes to the surface (accumulation), moderate positive bias pushes them away (depletion), and beyond threshold electrons gather in a thin inversion layer. Those electrons come from a port holding them at ground beside the oxide, the 1D stand-in for a transistor's source and drain; without it, they'd have to diffuse in from the back contact, which takes minutes.

φ (dotted) is drawn in the silicon and across the oxide, to show what it is and what it isn't. In the oxide, which holds no mobile charge, it's the only line there is, and its slope is the oxide field. In the silicon the band edges ride on it, V°i = φ + μ°i/(ziF), so it bends with them. But where it sits is a convention. This page anchors it 0.3 V below the silicon's conduction band edge and steps it down 0.3 V into the oxide; the library's own data would put it on the band edge, with no step. Any choice gives the same physics, once the gate's zeroCharge (the gate's Fermi level minus φ beside it, when the gate is uncharged) changes to match: the capacitance comes out the same to every digit. And φ doesn't meet the gate's Fermi level: a metal has no φ worth comparing, so the line just stops.

The capacitance at 1 kHz follows the ideal low-frequency C–V curve (dashed: the oxide in series with the silicon's dQ/dψ, from the closed-form surface charge), to four digits. Flat band is at VFB = zeroCharge + dipole + φbulk: each term depends on the anchoring, but their sum doesn't, and it's set by the gate interface (a work-function difference). Threshold is where the surface has inverted as strongly as the bulk is doped, ψs = 2φF. More doping widens the window between them and deepens the dip.
