MOS capacitor: accumulation, depletion, inversion

p-type silicon under 5 nm of oxide and a metal gate. The gate voltage sets the gate metal's Fermi level; the silicon's bands bend at the surface to meet it. Negative bias pulls holes to the surface (accumulation), moderate positive bias pushes them away (depletion), and beyond threshold electrons gather in a thin inversion layer. Those electrons come from a port holding them at ground beside the oxide, the 1D stand-in for a transistor's source and drain; without it, they'd have to diffuse in from the back contact. The capacitance at 1 kHz then follows the low-frequency C–V curve.
