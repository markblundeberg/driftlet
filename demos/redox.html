A redox couple at an electrode: cyclic voltammetry

Fe³⁺ + e⁻ ⇌ Fe²⁺ at platinum, in 0.5 M KCl, through a 20 µm stagnant layer to a stirred bulk. The diagram shows the platinum's Fermi level and the couple's redox level, the electronic level at which the local Fe³⁺/Fe²⁺ mixture is in equilibrium. The gap between them at the surface is the overpotential, which drives Butler–Volmer kinetics; the slope of the redox level across the layer is concentration polarisation. Slow scans give the steady, sigmoidal wave of a convective electrode; fast ones outrun diffusion and give peaks. A small rate constant pulls the waves apart.
