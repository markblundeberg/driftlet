driftlet demos

Each page builds its device with driftlet/kit, solves it in your browser, and redraws as you move the controls. The level diagram is the centrepiece: species voltages, band edges, Fermi and redox levels, across the device. Every page shows its whole script at the bottom, as a worked example to copy.
