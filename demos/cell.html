Ag | AgNO₃ | Ag: concentration polarization, live

10 mM silver nitrate between two silver electrodes, 20 µm apart. Ag⁺ is plated and stripped at the electrodes; NO₃⁻ is blocked. Step the cell voltage and watch the salt profile and current relax (adaptive BDF2, a fixed compute budget per animation frame). The impedance spectrum is the bulk resistance plus a finite-length Warburg arc from salt diffusion.
