Ag | AgNO₃ | Ag: concentration polarisation, live

10 mM silver nitrate between two silver electrodes, 20 µm apart, as a strictly neutral electrolyte. Ag⁺ is plated and stripped at the electrodes; NO₃⁻ is blocked. Step the cell voltage and watch the salt pile up at one electrode and deplete at the other, and the current relax toward its steady value. In the diagram, Ag⁺'s species voltage drops from one electrode to the other, while NO₃⁻'s, carrying no current once it's settled, goes level. The impedance spectrum at rest is the bulk resistance plus a finite-length Warburg arc from salt diffusion.
