Silicon solar cell under light

A thin n⁺ emitter on a thick p base. Light is a reservoir: the reaction photon → e⁻ + h⁺, with the photons' μ far above the gap, generates pairs uniformly. Carriers made within a diffusion length of the junction are collected, so the short-circuit current is about qG(Ln + Lp + W). Under light the quasi-Fermi levels split even at zero bias; at open circuit they split by qVoc and no current flows.
