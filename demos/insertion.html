Lithiating an intercalation host at constant current

A 2 µm host layer (strictly neutral, with Li⁺ + e⁻ as one neutral combination) takes Li⁺ from the electrolyte on the left and electrons from a current collector on the right. Its OCV is a regular solution with interaction Ω. The collector's voltage against Li is solved for as the current is held fixed. At high rates, lithium piles up at the surface and the voltage drops early.
