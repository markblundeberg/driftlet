Double layer at a blocking electrode: dilute vs crowded ions

0.1 M salt against an electrode held ψ above the bulk. Ideal (Poisson–Boltzmann) ions pile up without limit; on a lattice of finite size (Bikerman / Kilic–Bazant–Ajdari statistics) the counter-ions saturate and the layer thickens instead, so the charge grows as √ψ rather than exponentially.
