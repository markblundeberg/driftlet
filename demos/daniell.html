A Daniell cell discharging through a load

Zn | 0.1 M ZnSO₄ | separator | 0.1 M CuSO₄ | Cu, each solution 0.5 mm deep and still, the separator passing only sulfate. At open circuit each metal's Fermi level sits at its couple's redox level (Zn²⁺/Zn and Cu²⁺/Cu), and the cell voltage is the 1.10 V between them. Connect a load and current flows: each metal departs from its couple's level by an overpotential, the redox levels tilt across the electrolyte (ohmic drop, and the compositions changing near each electrode), and the voltage left for the load shrinks. As zinc dissolves and copper plates, sulfate crosses the separator to keep each side neutral.
